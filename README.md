# Humm

Humm is a demo of a speech training game developed by Texas A&M University with Brazos Valley Rehabilitation Center. It helps children practice speech sounds using a live mirror: the camera tracks the mouth, shows how each sound is made, and playful props react when the child gets it right.

**Try it.** Open this page in Safari or Chrome, tap Start, and allow the camera and microphone. Works best on an iPad or phone. Pick a sound (F, T, D, or V), watch the demonstration, then copy it.

**Privacy.** All camera and microphone processing happens on your device. No video or audio is recorded or sent anywhere.

Humm is a research prototype, not a medical device. Questions: Texas A&M Game Lab.
